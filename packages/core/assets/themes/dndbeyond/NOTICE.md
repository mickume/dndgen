# Third-party material in the dndbeyond theme

- `theme.css` and `browser-print.css` are adapted from the 5ePHB theme of
  [the Homebrewery](https://github.com/naturalcrit/homebrewery), MIT License,
  Copyright (c) 2016 Scott Tolksdorf.
- `img/` holds the Homebrewery's theme images (parchment, borders, footer accent, cover
  banner, dividers), from the same project. `PHB_footerAccentLeft.png` is a mirrored copy.
- `fonts/` holds Solbera's recreations of the fifth-edition fonts (Bookinsanity, Scaly Sans,
  Mr Eaves Small Caps, Nodesto Caps Condensed, Solbera Imitation), licensed under
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), as distributed with the
  Homebrewery, and Overpass Medium (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

This theme is unofficial and not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast or D&D Beyond.
